The OpenClaw YouTube channel has been busy this week. Five new videos dropped covering everything from first-principles explainers to security deep-dives and real daily workflows. Whether you're brand new to OpenClaw or a seasoned operator looking to sharpen your setup, there's something here for you.

## What is OpenClaw? Inside AI Agents, LLMs and the Agentic Loop

[![Watch on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/L7FF8Zgab3M/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L7FF8Zgab3M)

If you've been wondering how OpenClaw actually works under the hood, this is the video to start with. It covers what OpenClaw is, how it positions itself at the top of the agentic loop, and how large language models provide the underlying reasoning that powers your agents. A clean, jargon-light entry point for newcomers who want the big picture before diving into configuration.

## Chapter 10 - OpenClaw Security and Risks

[![Watch on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/BBKeBwqWQ_U/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BBKeBwqWQ_U)

Security is the topic everyone in the community is talking about right now, and this video addresses it head-on. The chapter covers OpenClaw's security model end-to-end: tool permissions, exec approvals, sandboxing strategies, and what happens when you run untrusted or public-facing agents. Given the ongoing community conversations around agent safety (including today's Telegram thread on Hacker News), this is extremely timely viewing.

## OpenClaw Explained: Why the Hype is (Mostly) Wrong

[![Watch on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/LlcdY1LA8Qw/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LlcdY1LA8Qw)

A refreshingly honest take on the AI agent hype cycle. If you've been burned by over-hyped AI tooling before, this critical perspective is worth your time before you commit to a deep integration.

## How I Use OpenClaw Daily

[![Watch on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/CxErCGVo-oo/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxErCGVo-oo)

Theory is one thing — seeing a real workflow in action is another. This practical walkthrough shows exactly how one power user structures their daily OpenClaw usage: which skills they load, how they handle memory across sessions, and tips for keeping context useful over time without it ballooning out of control. Highly recommended for anyone who's set up OpenClaw but isn't sure if they're using it efficiently.

## CORE CONCEPTS: Skills and Image Gen

[![Watch on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/K6CO0S5ql_A/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K6CO0S5ql_A)

A tutorial-style breakdown of two of OpenClaw's most powerful but sometimes misunderstood systems: Skills and the image generation pipeline. The video covers how to install skills, what goes in a `SKILL.md` file, and how to wire up `image_generate` inside your agents for dynamic visual output. Concrete and hands-on — exactly the kind of walkthrough that saves you an hour of trial and error.
